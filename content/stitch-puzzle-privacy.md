Last updated: October 5, 2026.

Stitch Puzzle is a cross-stitch puzzle game made by Igor Varyvoda. It has no account. It does not ask for your name, your email or your location.

## Data Stitch Puzzle handles

- **Your progress.** Levels, coins, your cottage and your settings are saved in the game's private storage on your phone. Deleting the game deletes them.
- **Cloud backup, only if you turn it on.** In Settings you can make a backup code. The game then sends a copy of your game progress to the developer's server, stored under that code, and updates it after each win. It holds game data only, no personal information. Anyone who has your code can restore that progress, so keep it private.
- **Purchases.** Coin and help packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment. The game keeps the ids of delivered purchases so that a purchase is never delivered twice.
- **Ads.** An ad plays only when you choose to watch one, for example for a free continue. Ads come from Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier and device information to show and measure ads. See [how Google uses data in ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Notifications.** If you allow them, the game schedules local reminders on your phone (your daily gift, your cat). Nothing is sent through a server.
- **Analytics.** None.

## Your choices

You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings, and reset or limit your advertising identifier there. Removing the game removes everything it stored on the phone.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
