Last updated: October 9, 2026.

Stitch Puzzle is a cross-stitch puzzle game made by Igor Varyvoda. It has no account. It does not ask for your name, your email or your location.

## Data Stitch Puzzle handles

- **Your progress.** Levels, coins, your cottage and your settings are saved in the game's private storage on your phone. Deleting the game deletes them.
- **Cloud backup, only if you turn it on.** In Settings you can make a backup code. The game then sends a copy of your game progress to the developer's server, stored under that code, and updates it after each win. It holds game data only, no personal information. Anyone who has your code can restore that progress, so keep it private. To have a backup deleted, email the address below with its code.
- **Purchases.** Coin and help packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment. The game keeps the ids of delivered purchases on the phone so that a purchase is never delivered twice. **Those ids stay on the phone and are never uploaded, including in a cloud backup.**
- **Ads.** An ad plays only when you choose to watch one, for example for a free continue. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play, and the game is complete without ever watching one. Ads come from Google AdMob. According to [Google's own disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure), the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: your **IP address** (which may be used to estimate your device's general location — not your precise location, and the game never asks for location permission), **device and advertising identifiers**, **ads you have seen** and **app interactions** such as taps and video views, and **diagnostic information** such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage, including crash logs. That data goes to Google rather than to the developer; see [how Google uses data in ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Ad consent.** Before any ad is requested, and before the ads SDK starts at all, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer later under **Settings → Privacy choices**. On iPhone and iPad, iOS also asks separately before the app can use your advertising identifier for personalised ads; if you decline, you still get ads and rewards, they are simply less targeted.
- **Notifications.** If you allow them, the game schedules local reminders on your phone (your daily gift, your cat). Nothing is sent through a server.
- **Analytics.** None.

## Your choices

- Change your ad consent under Settings → Privacy choices.
- On iPhone and iPad, refuse or withdraw personalised ads in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.
- Turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
- Ask for a cloud backup to be deleted by emailing its code to the address below.
- Removing the game removes everything it stored on the phone.

## Children

Stitch Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and over, because it can show personalised ads where you have consented to them. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
