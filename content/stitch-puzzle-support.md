Stitch Puzzle is a calm puzzle game: send thread spools round the hoop to pull out the stitches of a picture, then decorate your cottage with the coins you earn.

## Common questions

**I'm stuck on a level.** Every level can be won without paying. Hold a spool to see which stitches it will pull before you tap it. The hint (it pulses after a few seconds) follows a winning order, and the game tells you when a move leads to a dead end, so you can undo it. Restarting a level is always free.

**I can't tell two thread colours apart.** Turn on **Chart symbols** in Settings. Each colour then carries its own cross-stitch symbol on the spools and on the board, including on the badge that shows which colour a knot is waiting for.

**What are the knots and the pins?** A knotted spool will not move until you have stitched a number of cells in another colour; its badge shows which colour and how many are left. A pin holds a cell until one of its neighbours is stitched. Tied spools always go out together.

**Where did today's daily picture go?** The daily picture changes at midnight, your own local time, and an unfinished one ends with its day. Finish it the same day to keep your streak.

**How do I move my progress to a new phone?** On the old phone, open Settings and tap "Back up my progress" to get a backup code. On the new phone, enter the code under "Restore".

**My backup failed, or it says it is paused.** Check your connection and tap "Try again". If it says the backup is paused because a purchase could not be saved yet, keep playing for a moment so the game can save, and the next backup will go through on its own.

**I bought something on another phone.** Purchases are coins and helps that are added to your progress. Back up your progress on the old phone and restore it on the new one.

**I bought a pack and it did not arrive.** It should appear by itself: the game checks with the store each time it starts and credits anything outstanding, and it never credits the same purchase twice. If it still has not arrived, email the address below with the pack and roughly when you bought it.

**Do I have to watch ads?** No. The only ads are ones you start yourself, in exchange for an extra slot when a level is lost. How do I change my ad consent? Settings → **Privacy choices**, and on iPhone and iPad also in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Where is the printable chart?** Finish a picture, then tap "Get the real chart" on the win card, or "Chart" under the picture in the albums. It has the symbols, a DMC colour legend with stitch counts, and the finished size on 14-count Aida.

**How do I get the cat?** Buy the cat bed in the craft room of your cottage. Feed your cat once a day for a small gift.

**Does it work offline?** Yes, apart from the cloud backup and the rewarded ads.

**Is anything uploaded?** Only your game progress, and only when you make a backup code. See the [privacy policy](/stitch-puzzle/privacy/).
