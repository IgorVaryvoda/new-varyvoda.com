## The short version

I built the site, published the content, handled SEO and advertising, and kept it running. It became profitable.

Google Search supplied most of the visitors. Panda changed the rankings, traffic collapsed, and the business stopped working.

## What I did

There was no separation between product, engineering, and marketing. I wrote code, changed the information architecture, watched search queries, sold advertising, fixed broken pages, and checked whether each change helped or hurt the business.

That feedback was immediate. A technical mistake could reduce traffic. A bad page could rank and still lose users. A revenue decision could make the product worse.

## What went wrong

The site depended on one source of traffic. I knew that in the abstract and still failed to build a serious alternative.

SEO was not the mistake. It made the business possible. The mistake was letting almost the entire business depend on rules controlled by Google.

## Where it is now

The site is gone. This page is a historical record, not a maintained product.

It belongs here because I had already built and operated a profitable internet business before joining Sirv or using AI coding tools. It also explains why I pay close attention to distribution and platform risk now.
