Easy to pick up. A little trickier to put down.

Send a spool around the hoop. It pulls out the stitches that match its colour. Thread left over waits on your shelf.

Keep a little room. Plan the order so your shelf doesn’t fill up. Hold a spool to preview its move before you commit.

Enjoy the finish. Clear the hoop and see the picture stitched back together. Every puzzle has a checked, winning solution.
