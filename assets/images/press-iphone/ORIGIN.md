# Press for iPhone assets

The icon comes from `press-ios/PressIOS/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png`.
The app screenshots come from `press-ios/goldie/out/raw/iphone-6.9/`, captured on 27 September 2026 and also used for the published App Store screenshots. They show the real app, with the simulator's demonstration photo library.

These are WebP conversions of those existing assets, with no generated UI or changed screenshot content. The screenshots are resized to 800px wide and the icon to 192px. Origin metadata is stored in a JSON sidecar beside each raster.
