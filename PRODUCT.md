# varyvoda.com

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Igor Varyvoda's personal site presents his work and writing. The Stitch Puzzle page promotes his iOS and Android game and links to its existing build article, support and privacy pages.

## Users

Inferred from the requested promo page: prospective players looking for a calm puzzle game, including cross-stitch hobbyists.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Hugo static site deployed by GitHub Actions over SFTP to Igor's server. Preserve the existing article and support/privacy routes. Store metadata must link to the game lander on varyvoda.com. Confirmed by the user: this should be a separate lander in the game's cozy style, with Impeccable design guidance.

## Brand Commitments

Use the game's real artwork and Nunito lettering. Preserve Igor's direct voice and factual product claims.

## Evidence on Hand

`content/posts/stitch-puzzle-reviews-first.md`, its screenshots in `assets/images/posts/`, and the sibling `stitch-puzzle` repository contain the source evidence. The current game has 616 puzzles in 77 albums, optional rewarded ads, free printable DMC charts, a cottage and a cat. No testimonials or rating claims have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Show the real game and make both store links easy to find.
- Explain the spool-and-shelf puzzle clearly.
- Preserve the article as the development story.
- Keep downloads, support and privacy accessible on mobile.
